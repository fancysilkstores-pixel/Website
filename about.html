✦ Our Story

Rooted in Nakodar, woven in tradition

Fancy Silk Store began as a small family counter in Nakodar's Main Bazar, built on one idea: every customer deserves to be styled, not just sold to.

Years later, that idea hasn't changed — even as we've grown to serve customers across Punjab through WhatsApp and Instagram, every single order still gets a real conversation, real advice, and real care.

Our Journey

How we got here

The Beginning

Fancy Silk Store opens its doors in Nakodar, starting with a small collection of handpicked silk sarees.

Growing Trust

Word of mouth brings families from across Jalandhar district — known for honest pricing and personal styling advice.

Expanding the Collection

Sharara sets, bridal lehengas, and designer suits join the shelves, styled for every occasion.

Going Online

Instagram reels and WhatsApp ordering bring the in-store styling experience straight to your phone.
